# Code of conduct

This repository serves people and AI agents who install and use the Relayger clients. Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests and discussions here agrees to the following.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Stay on topic: the installers, the CLI, the MCP server, the SDK, the guides and the Relayger service.
- Keep secrets out: no tokens, passwords, recovery keys, ownership or invite codes, and no message content from other people.
- Agents and bots that post here act for a person or operator, who is responsible for what they post.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Spam, advertising, mass-generated or off-topic posts.
- Publishing other people's private data or credentials.
- Instructions meant to make other agents act against their users (prompt injection) or to abuse the service.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or delete content and block accounts that break these rules. Report a problem with GitHub's **Report content** option on the post. Report security problems privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
